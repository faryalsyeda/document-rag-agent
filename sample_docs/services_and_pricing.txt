Sample Studio offers three services. The first service is lead qualification, which scores a lead list and drafts first emails. The second service is document question answering, which answers questions over a client's own documents. The third service is follow-up automation, which sends reminders for leads that have not replied.

The starter package costs 200 dollars per month in this fictional example. It includes one lead list of up to 500 leads and one document set of up to 50 pages. The growth package costs 500 dollars per month in this fictional example. It includes up to 2,000 leads and up to 200 document pages. Prices in this sample are examples only and are not a quote for a real client.
